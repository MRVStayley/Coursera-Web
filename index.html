Projects

Virtually all of my work is confidential, but some clients have formally recognised its value. "The work has helped give redacted client name significant competitive advantage in our sector"
The following are the last three projects I have worked on, with the client names redacted for confidentiality.

Private Client, Shipping - Qlik Developer

Working within the International Shipping industry, the client wanted to replace its legacy Qlik estate with a modern platform based on Snowflake and Thoughtspot AI.
Working with the Customer Experience, Data Governance and DBT teams, I was a central resource for interpreting the client's existing Business Logic and provided a simple yet robust data model for their developers.

NHS Client - Snowflake Developer

Full Migration of an NHS Trust's existing on-premise data warehouse via Microsoft Azure pipelines onto Snowflake. As Key Architect, my role was to develop procedures and strategies for the migration whilst training the Trust's team of five SQL Developers on cloud-based concepts and techniques.

Private Client, Legal - BI Developer

Development of a centralised Data Warhouse solution for the Client's Case Managment system. Central to this role was the provision of an MVC WebApp that simplified the ETL management processes and constrained diverse operational data into common Business Definitions. Once delivered, the information within the warehouse was used to gain insight into the client's extensive portfolio and to drive change in operational practices.
